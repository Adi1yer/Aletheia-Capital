### Arm 2: Momentum + Dividend Drip ✓ KEEP

**Description:** 80% Arm C momentum + 20% dividend ballast; dividends drip into growth quarterly

**Configuration:**
- Growth sleeve: 80% (Arm C momentum)

- Covered calls: None
- Drip mechanics: All dividends accumulate, deploy to growth at quarterly rebalance

**Verdict: ✓ KEEP** - Beats pure momentum on Sharpe across both primary windows. Better total returns without materially worse drawdowns.

**Why it works:**
- 20% dividend ballast provides steady income (~1.5-3% yield = 0.3-0.6% portfolio yield)
- Quarterly drip deployment buys more momentum names at rebalance
- No upside cap on growth names (critical difference vs buy-write strategies)
- Diversification benefit from dividend payers (utilities, staples) during tech selloffs

## Implementation Notes

### What Works

**Dividend Ballast Mechanics:**
- 20% allocation sufficient for meaningful income without diluting momentum
- Top 20 liquid dividend payers (≥1.5% yield) provides stable income stream
- Point-in-time selection (no hindsight) ensures citeable methodology
- Quarterly drip deployment aligns with Arm C rebalance cadence

**Income Drip Strategy:**
- Separate cash bucket for accumulated income (dividends + optional CC premiums)
- Deploy at scheduled quarterly rebalance (no discretionary timing)
- Buys additional growth names proportionally (maintains EW within growth sleeve)
- Simple, mechanical, auditable

**Critical Design Principle:**
- Growth names NEVER overwritten (avoids buy-write upside cap problem)
- Ballast-only CCs keep income generation separate from growth driver
- Honest framing: "income buys growth," not "CCs hedge drawdowns"

### Citeable Methodology

✓ **Pre-registered design** - all rules documented before running  
✓ **No hindsight bias** - point-in-time universes, IPO filters  
✓ **No survivorship bias** - dividend payers selected by yield at each rebalance  
✓ **Free data only** - yfinance for prices/dividends, synthetic BS for CCs  
✓ **Transparent costs** - 7 bps trading costs baked in  
✓ **Full audit trail** - every trade logged (type, ticker, quantity, price, sleeve)  
✓ **Stress tested** - 2000-2002 dot-com crash validated ballast benefit  

### Data & Assumptions

- **Price data:** yfinance (free)
- **Dividend data:** yfinance trailing 12-month dividends, point-in-time
- **CC pricing:** Synthetic Black-Scholes (realized vol * 1.1 as IV proxy)
- **Ballast universe:** Large-cap dividend payers (utilities, staples, pharma, telcos)
- **Trading costs:** 7 bps round-trip (conservative for paper brokerage)
- **Rebalance frequency:** Quarterly (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct)
- **Initial NAV:** $10,000
